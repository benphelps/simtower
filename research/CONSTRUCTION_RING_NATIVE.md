# Native per-floor construction ring

Direct IDA inspection2026-10-15: `47740` allocates a builder block for each native floor record, negates its type, assigns its per-floor tenant slot, writes countdown BYTE+17=12, initializes builders40E11, then calls3862B. Multi-storey placement wrappers call this separately and in their recovered allocation order: cinema/party below-first above ground, recycling/metro upper-first underground, cathedral bottom-first.

`3862B` has ten physical slots, a BYTE head/count, and parallel arrays containing floor, tenant registration and starting WORD tick. When full, it calls38680 on the oldest **record**, then appends the new record. Because builder allocation precedes enqueue, forced completion happens after the current part's allocation but before the next part's allocation. A Tower regression fills ten records with a clinic followed by offices, then constructs a metro: upper builders allocate at60; completing the old clinic frees0; middle builders then reuse0. Allocating all logical-room builders before enqueue produces a different cohort.

`387F1` scans the ring in order, decrements each record's BYTE+17 and writes dirty BYTE+13. At the first zero it calls38680 and immediately returns; later records keep their countdown that tick. Three records placed together complete at12,13,14 ticks, not together at12.38680 always completes the **head**, clears its builders40C2F, restores its positive type, clears its temporary tenant slot and countdown, and registers only that floor. Cinema2E172 splits only that floor's31cells into booth7 plus auditorium24; first completed booth creates the movie program and consumes one title RNG draw. The later floor joins the existing program without another title draw.

`385F6` force-completes **all** pending records in FIFO order. Its direct callers are daily parking300BB, daily medical2C3BF, commercial opening32024, show preparation2E3CF, cathedral dawnAB30, and emergency initialization17FE0. Consequently constructors placed just before dawn/240 opening finish immediately at that boundary; new actor records are initialized during each completion before subsequent daily budgets/cohort calls. Fire's17FE0 occurs after3010/3011 announcement acknowledgment, and bomb's after3021/3022 search briefing. These drain boundaries are explicitly Tower-tested. Topology-only parking306C5 and restaurant opening320F0 do not invoke the drain.

`original-construction-ring.js` stores `state.nativeConstructionRing` (`head/count/slots`) and `room.nativeConstructionParts` (`level/floor/type/completedType/remaining/dirty/completed/startedTick`). The former logical `construction` list/countdown is a derived compatibility/UI adapter. Completion changes only that level's actor block and per-floor registration. A completed cinema half exposes56 actors while its sibling's56 builders remain negative; pool and ring saves validate both states. Actor dispatch follows ascending global actor slots rather than completion/insertion order.

The partial completion also affects gameplay: only completed native records produce noise; the shared movie program is editable after the first booth exists. Recycling global5A678 counts centers at placement, whereas F530/F60E update only positive completed20/21 records. Cathedral base40 can update its completed native animation even while upper records remain pending. Dawn/evening dirty writes include completed ancillary levels independently. The renderer consumes pending dirty bytes only for visible floor spans, preserving offscreen construction updates.

Legacy browser queues lack physical per-floor history. Migration copies the logical remaining countdown into each native floor record and reconstructs its insertion order. More than ten reconstructed records force oldest completion to establish a valid native ring; lost historical countdown/forced-completion timing cannot be recovered from old files. Native save files remain deferred.

`tests/construction-ring-native.test.mjs` covers first-zero decrement boundaries, partial active actor pools, completion slot ordering, overflow reuse, signed/native counter effects, atomic corruption rejection and legacy migration. Existing cinema fixtures now wait for both record completions when asserting112 actors.
